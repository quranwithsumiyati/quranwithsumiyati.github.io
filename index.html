Story

A journey
still continuing

I grew up reciting the Qur'an with my father — a santri and Quran teacher in our village in Lebak. By 6th grade I had memorized Juz 30 and competed in the regency MTQ. By my early twenties I had completed the full 30 juz at LTIQ Assyifa Alkhoeriyyah, finishing a two-year program in one and a half.

Teaching followed naturally — at pesantrens, university institutions, through private tutoring, and online. A 2020 trip to Jordan brought me to Shaykh Idris for re-recitation and a sanad for Matan Jazariyyah. The desire to understand what I had been memorizing led me to Arabic, then to Tafsir — and eventually to Pakistan, where I completed my Master's degree in January 2026.

The further I travel on this path, the more I realize: being a Qur'an teacher means remaining a lifelong student.

Teman Murojaah

Finding a partner
to keep memorizing

Teman Murojaah was born from a personal struggle: finding it hard to maintain memorization alone. In July 2020, I launched a simple program — daily, weekly, and monthly murojaah with paired partners and accountability systems.

The program is free. Participants contribute a voluntary infak (from IDR 50,000) to fund guest speakers, Palestinian aid, and facilitator appreciation. The first batch had 30 participants.

We are now in Batch 17, having served approximately 1,500 participants across Indonesia and abroad — including a branch in Pakistan that continues to operate independently.

How it works

Anyone who has memorized at least 1 juz of the Quran and is committed to maintaining or adding to their memorization.
Each participant is paired with a murojaah partner. You submit your daily recitation and check in with your partner. Weekly group sessions and monthly evaluations provide structure and motivation.
New batches open periodically. Join the waiting list or follow announcements via WhatsApp. Contact me directly for the current batch status.
