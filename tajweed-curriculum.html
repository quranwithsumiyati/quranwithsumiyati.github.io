Kuasai Kaidah Ilmu Tajwid & Praktik Membaca Al-Qur'an

The Purpose of This Class

The Tajweed Class is designed to help students gradually understand the rules and principles of Tajweed and apply them correctly when reading the Qur’an. The lessons do not focus solely on theoretical understanding but also include direct Qur’an reading practice to improve and refine the accuracy of their recitation.

Through a combination of theory and practice, students will understand the reasoning behind different recitation rules, recognize how these rules are applied in Qur’anic verses, and develop the habit of reading the Qur’an according to the Tajweed rules they have learned. In this way, Tajweed is not merely understood as a set of theoretical rules, but becomes a practical guide that helps students read the Qur’an well and correctly.

Tujuan dari Kelas Ini

Kelas Tajwid merupakan kelas yang dirancang untuk membantu murid memahami kaidah-kaidah ilmu tajwid secara bertahap serta mampu menerapkannya dalam membaca Al-Qur’an. Pembelajaran tidak hanya berfokus pada pemahaman teori, tetapi juga disertai dengan praktik membaca Al-Qur’an secara langsung untuk memperbaiki bacaan.

Melalui pembelajaran teori dan praktik, murid akan memahami alasan di balik suatu hukum bacaan, mengenali penerapannya dalam ayat-ayat Al-Qur’an, serta membiasakan diri membaca sesuai dengan kaidah tajwid yang telah dipelajari. Dengan demikian, ilmu tajwid tidak hanya dipahami sebagai teori, tetapi menjadi pedoman yang membantu murid membaca Al-Qur’an dengan baik dan benar.

Chapter 01 Bab 01

The Virtues of Learning the Qur’an

In this chapter, students will study several Qur’anic ayah and hadiths about the virtues of engaging with the Qur’an. The discussion will cover the virtues of studying, reading, memorizing, teaching, and practicing the Qur’an.

Keutamaan Belajar Al-Qur'an

Pada bab ini, murid akan mempelajari beberapa ayat Al-Qur’an dan hadis tentang keutamaan berinteraksi dengan Al-Qur’an. Pembahasan mencakup keutamaan mempelajari, membaca, menghafal, mengajarkan, dan mengamalkan Al-Qur’an.

Chapter 02 Bab 02

Manners When Reading the Qur’an

In this chapter, students will learn about the proper manners (adab) that should be observed when reading and learning the Qur'an.

Adab Membaca Al-Qur'an

Pada bab ini, murid akan mempelajari apa saja adab yang harus diperhatikan ketika membaca dan belajar Al-Qur'an.

Chapter 03 Bab 03

Introduction to Tajwid of the Qur’an

In this chapter, students will learn the definition of tajwid, the evidence and legal basis underlying the importance of studying tajwid, as well as the types of mistakes commonly made when reading the Qur'an. This material aims to build students' fundamental understanding of why tajwid needs to be studied.

Pengantar Ilmu Tajwid

Pada bab ini, siswa akan mempelajari pengertian ilmu tajwid, dalil dan landasan hukum yang mendasari pentingnya mempelajari ilmu tajwid, serta jenis-jenis kesalahan yang sering terjadi dalam membaca Al-Qur'an. Materi ini bertujuan untuk membangun pemahaman dasar siswa mengenai mengapa ilmu tajwid perlu dipelajari.

Chapter 04 Bab 04

Seeking Refuge and Saying Basmallah (Isti'adzah & Basmallah)

In this section, students will learn the recitation of isti'adzah (ta'awudz) and basmalah, which are recited before beginning to read the Qur'an. Students will learn to pronounce both correctly, understand when each is recited, and recognize how to read them when connected to the beginning of a surah, whether through washal (continuous recitation) or waqaf (stopping).

Isti'adzah dan Basmallah

Pada bagian ini, siswa akan mempelajari bacaan isti'adzah (ta'awudz) dan basmalah, yaitu bacaan yang diucapkan sebelum memulai membaca Al-Qur'an. Siswa akan belajar melafalkan kedua bacaan tersebut dengan benar, memahami kapan keduanya dibaca, serta mengenali cara membacanya ketika bersambung dengan awal surah, baik dengan cara washal (disambung) maupun waqaf (dihentikan).

Milestone 1 Evaluation: Virtues, Adab, Introduction to Tajweed & Isti'adzah / Basmallah (Chapters 01–04)
Evaluasi Milestone 1: Keutamaan Al-Qur'an, Adab, Pengantar Ilmu Tajwid & Isti'adzah / Basmallah (Bab 01–04)
Chapter 05 Bab 05

The Articulation Point of the Letters (Makhraj Huruf)

In this section, students will learn about the makhraj huruf, which are the points of articulation where letters originate. Students will learn about the number of makhraj, both general and specific, as well as how to pronounce each letter correctly.

Makhroj Huruf

Pada bagian ini, siswa akan mempelajari makhraj huruf, yaitu tempat-tempat keluarnya huruf. Siswa akan belajar mengenai jumlah makhraj huruf baik secara umum maupun spesifik, serta belajar bagaimana cara mengucapkannya.

Chapter 06 Bab 06

The Rules of Shamsiyyah and Qamariyyah “Al” (Lam Ta’rif)

In this chapter, students will learn about alif lam qamariyah and syamsiyah, including their characteristics and how to read them.

Lam Ta’rif Shamsiyyah dan Qamariyyah

Pada bab ini, siswa akan belajar tentang alif lam qamariyah dan syamsiyah, apa saja ciri-cirinya, dan bagaimana cara membacanya.

🎯 Checkpoint 2
Milestone 2 Evaluation: Makhraj Huruf & Lam Ta'rif Shamsiyyah / Qamariyyah (Chapters 05–06)
Evaluasi Milestone 2: Makhroj Huruf & Lam Ta’rif Shamsiyyah / Qamariyyah (Bab 05–06)
Chapter 07 Bab 07

The Noon Sakinah and Tanween Rules

In this chapter, students will learn the rulings of nun sukun and tanwin when they meet certain Hijaiyah letters, namely izhar, idgham, iqlab, and ikhfa. Students will learn to recognize the letters included in each ruling, understand how to read them, and apply them when reading verses of the Qur'an.

Hukum Nun Sukun dan Tanwin

Pada bab ini, siswa akan mempelajari hukum bacaan nun sukun dan tanwin ketika bertemu dengan huruf-huruf hijaiyah, yaitu izhar, idgham, iqlab, dan ikhfa. Siswa akan belajar mengenali huruf-huruf yang termasuk dalam setiap hukum bacaan tersebut, memahami cara membacanya masing-masing, serta berlatih menerapkannya saat membaca kata atau ayat Al-Qur'an.

Chapter 08 Bab 08

The Miim Sakinah Rules

In this chapter, students will learn the rulings of meem sukun when it meets certain Hijaiyah letters, namely idgham mitslain, ikhfa syafawi, and izhar syafawi. Students will learn to recognize the letters included in each ruling, understand how to read them, and apply them when reading verses of the Qur'an.

Hukum Mim Sukun

Pada bab ini, siswa akan mempelajari hukum bacaan mim sukun ketika bertemu dengan huruf-huruf hijaiyah, yaitu idgham mitslain, ikhfa syafawi, dan izhar syafawi. Siswa akan belajar mengenali huruf-huruf yang termasuk dalam setiap hukum bacaan tersebut, memahami cara membacanya, dan menerapkannya saat membaca ayat Al-Qur'an.

Chapter 09 Bab 09

The Rules of Elongation (Madd)

In this section, students will learn the definition of mad, the classification of mad, the causes of each type of mad, as well as the number of harakat read in each mad ruling. Students will also practice applying these mad readings in verses of the Qur'an.

Mad

Pada bagian ini, siswa akan mempelajari pengertian mad, pembagian mad, sebab-sebab terjadinya setiap jenis mad, serta jumlah harakat yang dibaca pada setiap hukum mad. Siswa juga akan berlatih menerapkan bacaan mad tersebut dalam ayat Al-Qur'an.

🎯 Checkpoint 3
Milestone 3 Evaluation: Noon Sakinah & Tanween, Miim Sakinah & Madd Rules (Chapters 07–09)
Evaluasi Milestone 3: Hukum Nun Sukun & Tanwin, Mim Sukun & Hukum Mad (Bab 07–09)
Chapter 10 Bab 10

The Characteristics of the Letters (Sifat al-Huruf)

In this section, students will learn the definition of Sifat al-Huruf (the characteristics or qualities of Arabic letters), which describe how their sounds are produced. These include audible and physical characteristics such as heaviness and lightness, voiced and whispered sounds, strength and softness, and bouncing effects. Students will also practice identifying and applying these characteristics when reciting the Qur'an.

Sifat Huruf

Pada bagian ini, murid akan mempelajari pengertian sifat huruf, pembagian sifat huruf yang memiliki lawan dan sifat huruf yang tidak memiliki lawan, serta karakteristik masing-masing sifat. Murid juga akan berlatih mengenali dan mempraktikkan sifat-sifat huruf dalam bacaan Al-Qur'an.

Chapter 11 Bab 11

Tafkhim & Tarqiq (Heavy / Light Pronunciation)

In this section, students will learn the definitions of Tafkhim (heavy pronunciation) and Tarqiq (light pronunciation). Students will learn to identify the letters pronounced heavily and lightly, understand the rules governing the pronunciation of Ra’ (ر) as either heavy or light, and practice applying these rules correctly when reciting the Qur’an.

Tafkhim dan Tarqiq

Pada bagian ini, murid akan mempelajari pengertian tafkhim dan tarqiq, mengenali huruf-huruf yang dibaca tebal dan tipis, serta memahami ketentuan bacaan ra' (ر) yang dibaca tebal maupun tipis. Murid juga akan berlatih menerapkan hukum bacaan tafkhim dan tarqiq dalam ayat-ayat Al-Qur'an.

Chapter 12 Bab 12

Idhgom (Assimilation) — The Rules of Mithlayn, Mutaqaaribayn, and Mutajaanisayn

In this section, students will learn the definition of Idgham and its classification based on the relationship between the two letters involved in the merging process. It is divided into three main categories: Idgham Mutamatsilain, Idgham Mutajanisain, and Idgham Mutaqaribain. Students will also learn the rules and pronunciation of each type and practice applying them when reciting verses of the Qur'an.

Idgham (Mutamatsilain, Mutajanisain, dan Mutaqaribain)

Pada bagian ini, murid akan mempelajari pengertian Idgham dan pengelompokannya berdasarkan hubungan antara dua huruf, yaitu Idgham Mutamatsilain, Idgham Mutajanisain, dan Idgham Mutaqaribain. Murid juga akan memahami ketentuan dan cara membaca masing-masing jenis idgham serta berlatih membacanya.

Chapter 13 Bab 13

Waqf (The Rules of Stopping)

In this section, students will learn the concept of Waqf (pausing) in Qur'anic recitation, recognizing the various Waqf symbols found in the Mushaf and understanding the rules of stopping and resuming recitation (Ibtida'). Students will also explore the different types of Waqf, including where and why to pause and how to properly apply each rule. Through this study, students will develop the ability to pause and resume their recitation correctly while preserving the clarity and completeness of the Qur'anic meanings.

Waqf

Pada bagian ini, murid akan mempelajari pengertian Waqf, mengenali berbagai tanda waqaf dalam Al-Qur'an, serta memahami ketentuan berhenti dan memulai kembali bacaan (ibtida'). Murid juga akan mempelajari jenis-jenis waqaf dan cara penerapannya agar dapat membaca Al-Qur'an dengan tepat serta memperhatikan kesempurnaan makna ayat.

🎯 Checkpoint 4
Milestone 4 Evaluation: Sifat al-Huruf, Tafkhim / Tarqiq, Idhgom & Waqf Rules (Chapters 10–13)
Evaluasi Milestone 4: Sifat Huruf, Tafkhim / Tarqiq, Idgham & Kaidah Waqf (Bab 10–13)
Chapter 14 Bab 14

Hamzatul Wasl and Hamzatul Qath

In this section, students will learn the definitions of Hamzatul Wasl and Hamzatul Qat‘, identify their written forms in the Mushaf, and understand the differences between them. Students will also learn the pronunciation rules of both types and practice applying them in Qur'anic recitation.

Hamzah Wasol dan Hamzah Qotho’

Pada bagian ini, murid akan mempelajari pengertian Hamzatul Wasl dan Hamzatul Qat‘, mengenali bentuk penulisannya dalam mushaf Al-Qur'an, serta memahami perbedaan keduanya. Murid juga akan mempelajari ketentuan pelafalan hamzah ketika memulai dan menyambung bacaan, serta berlatih membacanya.

Chapter 15 Bab 15

Uncommon Recitations (Gharibul Qur’an)

In this section, students will learn about Gharibul Qur’an, which refers to special or uncommon words in the Qur’an that have unique pronunciation rules differing from standard Tajweed patterns. Students will identify these words, understand their specific pronunciation rules, and practice reciting them correctly.

Ghoribul Qur'an

Pada bagian ini, murid akan mempelajari lafaz-lafaz khusus dalam Al-Qur’an yang memiliki cara baca berbeda dari kaidah umum tajwid. Murid akan mengenali bentuk lafaz, memahami ketentuan cara membacanya, serta berlatih membacanya.
