✅ Prerequisite: Completed Tahsin & Passed Recitation Assessment

Ziyadah (New Memorization)

This program is designed for students who wish to memorize the Qur’an. To join this program, students must have previously completed Tahsin (Qur’an recitation improvement) and passed a recitation assessment.

Students will be guided by qualified female Qur’an teachers (Ustazah) to complete the memorization of all 30 Juz of the Qur’an. Various study schedules are available, allowing students to choose a schedule that best suits their ability and availability.

Frequently Asked Questions — Tahfiz Program Pertanyaan Seputar Tahfiz Program

Everything you need to know about memorization targets, Sambung Ayat quality checks, and what makes this program unique.

Penjelasan lengkap mengenai target hafalan, ujian sambung ayat per juz, dan keunggulan pendampingan program tahfiz kami.

Students can still continue with the Tahfiz Program even if they are unable to meet the set memorization targets.

The targets are simply guidelines to help students estimate how long it may take them to complete the memorization of all 30 Juz of the Qur’an and how many pages they need to memorize within a certain period.

However, each student's progress is adjusted according to their individual ability and circumstances. Students can continue memorizing at their own pace without feeling pressured by the targets.

Peserta tetap dapat melanjutkan program tahfiz meskipun belum mencapai target yang telah ditentukan.

Pada dasarnya, target hafalan dibuat sebagai panduan untuk membantu murid memperkirakan waktu yang dibutuhkan untuk menyelesaikan hafalan 30 juz serta mengetahui jumlah halaman yang perlu dihafalkan dalam periode tertentu.

Namun, pencapaian target tetap disesuaikan dengan kemampuan dan kondisi masing-masing peserta. Murid dapat terus melanjutkan hafalan sesuai dengan kemampuan dan progresnya, tanpa harus merasa terbebani oleh target yang telah ditetapkan.

To maintain the quality of memorization, every student who completes one Juz will take a Qur’an continuation test (Sambung Ayat).

In this test, students will be given four questions consisting of selected verses that they must continue reciting from memory. If they are able to answer all four questions correctly and fluently, they may proceed to memorizing the next Juz.

However, if they are unable to answer the questions correctly, they will need to retake the test until they pass.

In this way, the quality and strength of memorization remain our main priorities before students move on to the next Juz.

Untuk menjaga kualitas hafalan, setiap peserta yang telah menyelesaikan satu juz akan mengikuti ujian sambung ayat.

Dalam ujian ini, peserta akan diberikan empat soal berupa potongan ayat yang harus dilanjutkan. Jika peserta mampu menjawab seluruh soal dengan baik dan benar, peserta diperbolehkan melanjutkan hafalan ke juz berikutnya.

Namun, jika peserta belum mampu menjawab dengan baik dan benar, peserta perlu mengulang ujian hingga dinyatakan lulus.

Dengan demikian, kualitas dan kekuatan hafalan tetap menjadi perhatian utama sebelum peserta melanjutkan ke juz berikutnya.

This program is designed to guide students through their Qur’an memorization journey step by step, with realistic targets tailored to each student's individual abilities.

Each student will receive direct guidance from a female Qur’an teacher (Ustazah), regular progress evaluations, and support in maintaining the quality of their memorization through revision sessions (Murojaah) and Qur’an continuation tests.

Our focus is not only on helping students complete the memorization of all 30 Juz, but also on ensuring the accuracy of their recitation, the strength of their memorization, and their consistency in engaging with the Qur’an.

Through this approach, students can experience a more structured, measurable, and personalized memorization journey at their own pace.

Program ini dirancang untuk mendampingi peserta menghafal Al-Qur'an secara bertahap dengan target yang realistis dan disesuaikan dengan kemampuan masing-masing.

Setiap peserta akan mendapatkan bimbingan langsung dari ustazah, evaluasi perkembangan hafalan secara berkala, serta pendampingan dalam menjaga kualitas hafalan melalui program murojaah dan ujian sambung ayat.

Kami tidak hanya berfokus pada pencapaian hafalan 30 juz, tetapi juga memperhatikan ketepatan bacaan, kekuatan hafalan, dan konsistensi peserta dalam berinteraksi dengan Al-Qur'an.

Dengan demikian, setiap peserta dapat menjalani proses menghafal dengan lebih terarah, terukur, dan sesuai dengan kemampuan masing-masing.
