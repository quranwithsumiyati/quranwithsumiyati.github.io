Pre-Tajweed Class Curriculum

This class aims to help students read the Qur'an well, correctly, and fluently. Students focus on practicing direct hands-on improvement of their recitation, covering makhraj, the length of recitation, waqaf (stopping), and special readings in the Qur'an.

Instructor: Sumiyati Classes: 18 Classes (1-6 Free) Checkpoints: 2 Milestone Reviews Session: 40 Mins / Class

The Purpose of This Class

Objectives
This class aims to help students read the Qur'an well, correctly, and fluently. Students will focus on practicing direct hands-on improvement of their recitation, covering makhraj (points of articulation), the length of recitation, waqaf (stopping), and special readings in the Qur'an, without needing to study tajwid theory in depth beforehand.

🎯 Who This Class Is For

Adult learners, students, and beginners looking to correct pronunciation habits, transition smoothly from basic Iqra'/Qiroati to the Mushaf, or build a rock-solid hands-on foundation before taking in-depth Tajwid theory.

📋 Prerequisites

Basic ability to recognize isolated Hijaiyah letters. No memorization of complex tajwid rules or Arabic grammar required beforehand.
